# Four world blueprints | Solved Studio Edition 001

## World 1: Application Alley
**Purpose:** introduce move/jump/dash and the three skill types. Bright blue skyline, warm terracotta brick under grassy tops, cute signs, coffee shop, vines and flowering trees. Gameplay: stepping stones, short pits, gentle enemy patrols, optional upper-route secret. CV-bot and angry inbox foes. Boss: CV Filter, a friendly-ironic recruiting robot with a readable tell/dodge/stomp loop. Checkpoint before boss. Exit opens the product-building challenge.

## World 2: Ship It City
**Purpose:** demonstrate execution under scope and time constraints. Golden sunset factory district, cranes, ducts, tool crates, moving lifts and hazard-trimmed steel. Gameplay: vertical climb, toggle machinery, short moving platforms, optional build-token detour. Boss: Scope Creep, whose arena extends or shifts sections until the player finishes objectives rather than collecting endless requirements. Checkpoint and safe re-entry.

## World 3: Growth Run
**Purpose:** showcase growth/creative launch judgement. Magenta-blue twilight, cyan monorail, rooftop signs, purple tech tiles and moving drone threats. Gameplay: momentum routes, dash-lane challenges, high/low paths with different pickups, flying paper-plane enemies. Boss: The Algorithm, telegraphed waves and speed opportunities. Avoid fake claims about business metrics.

## World 4: Final Interview HQ
**Purpose:** confident, human ending and portfolio discovery. Sunrise glasshouse with plants, pale stone, gold trim and cyan screens. Gameplay: brief precision ascent with friendly checkpoints, then an interactive interview with authentic portfolio evidence. Finish with real links and a genuine, contactable call-to-action. Do **not** simulate a real employment offer or represent an actual founder as endorsing the project.

## Edition configurability
Keep company/role/name/theme/dialogue/portfolio evidence in data rather than the game engine. Other employer editions should reuse movement, enemies, components and structure without pretending to have employer endorsement.

## Level acceptance
Every world needs a distinct entry, playable traversal, skill collectible, optional secret, checkpoint, boss or meaningful finale, end transition, completion metrics and a keyboard + touch test. Maintain 4–6 minute target for a first playthrough; always offer skip-to-portfolio for busy employers.
