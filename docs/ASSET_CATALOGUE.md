# Visual asset catalogue

See `assets/manifest.json` for machine-readable asset IDs, roles and paths.

**World 1 (Application Alley):** bright blue sky, distant lilac office blocks, elevated railway, green canopy and pink blossoms. Foreground: terracotta brick, lush grass, ladders, coffee facade and CV-themed enemies.

**World 2 (Ship It City):** warm sunset, cranes and factory rooftops. Foreground: steel plates, tool crates, moving platforms and machinery hazards.

**World 3 (Growth Run):** violet/magenta twilight, elevated cyan transport, neon data architecture. Foreground: cyan and magenta technological tiles, dash routes and paper-plane threats.

**World 4 (Final Interview HQ):** calm sunrise through a glass atrium, pale stone, gold trim, trees and flowers. Foreground: polished pale stone, glass and interview/portfolio interaction.

**Components:** `assets/tiles/` has 16 named terrain PNGs; `assets/props/` has 16 named prop PNGs. The `assets/production/characters/hero-atlas.png` and `assets/production/enemies/enemy-atlas.png` are art source candidates, not verified final frame definitions.

**Approved concept boards:** `references/approved-gameplay-target.png`, `augustus-character-concepts.png`, `enemies-hud-concepts.png` and `environment-concepts.png`. Keep them for visual comparison and don't conflate concept images with actual engine screenshots.

**Quality:** crisp nearest-neighbour pixel clusters; clear contrasting foreground; tile anchors separate from colliders; working animation states; no HUD blocks placed across critical jumps.
